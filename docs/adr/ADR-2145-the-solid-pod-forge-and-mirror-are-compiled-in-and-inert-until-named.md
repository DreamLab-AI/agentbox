# ADR-2145 — The solid-pod forge and mirror are compiled into the native pod and inert until relays and follows are named

## Context
- VisionFlow ADR-2019 decided that shipped agentbox pods run the solid-pod-rs forge and the kind-30618 mirror, with open edit, mainnet and any external follow list staying opt-in. solid-pod-rs ADR-2010 records the implementation and its hardening.
- Agentbox builds `solid-pod-rs-server` from a pinned source revision (`lib/solid-pod-rs.nix`, vendored lockfile), not from crates.io, and was on `0.5.0-alpha.13`.
- The forge and mirror had no manifest surface, the server read none of their settings in the old pin, and behind the Cloudflare Tunnel the pod now needs an explicit public origin for NIP-98 URL matching (solid-pod-rs security review M1).

## Decision
- Pin `solid-pod-rs` at `v0.5.0-alpha.17` (`55bfec1f`), with the source hash computed from the tag's tarball and the lockfile copied from the tag. The Git provenance patch still matches exactly once.
- Build with `forge-full` unless `[integrations.solid_pod_rs.forge] enable = false`. The pod then serves the forge under `/forge` and starts the mirror at boot.
- The `[integrations.solid_pod_rs.forge]` block carries: `chain` (default `txbt4`), `allow_mainnet` (false), `open_edit` (false), `private_repos` (false), `anchoring_ui` (true), `mirror_relays` (the embedded relay `ws://127.0.0.1:7777`), `mirror_follow` (empty) and `announce_relays` (empty). The launcher turns them into the server's `FORGE_*` environment.
- `mirror_follow` takes `<64-hex pubkey>/<repo>` or `self/<repo>`; `self` expands at boot to this container's sovereign key (public half, read from the identity file). The estate relay and any external follow entry go in the root `.env` (`AGENTBOX_FORGE_MIRROR_RELAYS`, `AGENTBOX_FORGE_MIRROR_FOLLOW`, `AGENTBOX_FORGE_ANNOUNCE_RELAYS`), because tracked files are public.
- The pod runs with `--trust-proxy` (manifest `trust_proxy`, default true) and `--public-base-url` set from `SOLID_POD_PUBLIC_URL`.
- The forge never broadcasts or spends. The anchoring trail key is generated by the forge with the OS RNG and kept in its own 0600 file; it is not the sovereign identity.

## Consequences
- The image gains relay egress and a remote-influenced `git fetch` in its default build, doing nothing until a follow entry exists. The operator must add `self/<repo>` or pubkey entries to use the mirror.
- Behaviour change: NIP-98 verification now matches the URL against the configured public origin, not whatever `Host` the proxy forwards. A wrong `base_url` breaks pod authentication; it was already the value baked into the image.
- The follow list is deliberately left documented and unpopulated (owner decision, 2026-10-20): `/forge` is served on the agentbox pod only, and nothing is mirrored until the owner adds entries. VisionClaw gets no `/forge` mount; the two are bound through the pod.
- Not done here: the image is not rebuilt (host shell, `rebuild`), so `activation_status` stays staged. The Nix expression was written without a local `nix`, so its first evaluation happens at that rebuild; the shell it generates is reviewed but not executed.
- The manifest schema now recognises the forge and proxy settings. The forge and proxy gates are catalogued as rebuild-class; individual forge policy booleans remain subordinate to the forge gate.
