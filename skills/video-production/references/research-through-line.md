# Research through-line

This reference preserves transferable decisions and their limits without carrying a historical production brief into new productions. Research inspected 23 October 2026. The evidence comes from archived experiments in the originating private research repository; nothing here needs that repository, its archive or its production assets to run.

Sources distinguish a retrieved paper or vendor capability, a local compatible installation, an executed operation, a verified property and an accepted film. Neither a universally preferred model nor an accepted autonomous end-to-end production was demonstrated.

| Geometry and camera are editable production inputs | Changes to massing, scale and projected camera motion improved specific views. | Seven guide, blur and mask variants did not repair sparse massing; world-space camera speed failed to predict a rapid screen-space exit. Camera and anchor changed together in one improvement. | Measure silhouette, parallax, support and projected motion. Revise the common world and dependent views; describe multivariable comparisons honestly. REQ-002/005/014. |
| Dense styling can preserve authored repeated states | Thirty-eight complete states expanded to 75 delivery frames with exact cadence; a three-state repair retained unaffected states. | Centre-detection error had a native-control floor; material variation remained. A camera moving on ones reduced savings despite held body poses. | Cache complete authored states, including camera and animated appearance; qualify dense styling alongside video generation. Cadence is not a texture score. REQ-008/014. |
| Identity and structural control are separate | Bounded native-view edits improved particular faces and props and preserved outside-alpha pixels. | Global references mixed anatomy; a restored face still smeared during motion. Numerical position prompts did not establish geometric conformance. | Supply identity references separately from world placement; inspect changed views and moving entrances. REQ-003/005/013. |
| Anchors are local evidence, not continuity guarantees | Timed guides improved some nearby appearances and an event endpoint. | Intermediate frames still morphed; tiled and raw-output controls rejected compression and tiling as sufficient explanations for another failure. | Use actual source-world views for spatial anchors; inspect between them and retain failed controls. Do not increase anchor density blindly. REQ-005/014. |
| Different masks make different claims | Generated tracking and guarded integer composites executed; lossless outside-alpha equality was verified. | Regional attention did not guarantee identity isolation; broad masks introduced anatomy defects or halos, and missing tracks could not justify borrowed frames. | Separate native ownership, projected bounds, attention or sampling masks, generated segmentation and final alpha, each with coordinates, clock and lineage. REQ-005/010/012. |
| Dialogue replacement is a dependency problem | Sample-indexed PCM replacement preserved samples outside the replacement union; guided crop alignment passed a bounded sequence. | Audio-only conditioning changed every generated frame in its clip; high AAC correlation missed unrelated PCM changes; acceptable alignment still had late mouth onset. | Keep native mouth scope, audio-sample scope, render ranges, generated clips and review cuts distinct. Fixed-clock rebake must inspect the whole edit. REQ-007/008/009/013. |
| Physical evidence needs a complete causal chain | A current-scale coupled solve and replay passed bounded length, contact and clock checks. | An attachment metric passed while cable length changed substantially; whole-scene collision and generated grips remained open. A complete 750-frame film passed delivery checks yet failed fine grips. | Verify intent, solve, replay, native guide and delivered picture separately; state telemetry and scene scope; recalibrate after scale or timing changes. REQ-006/013. |
| Exact graphics need an authoritative source layer | Known print was projected and composited with explicit unknown regions and occlusion. | Generation changed a card into a book and corrupted print; native projection did not guarantee alignment with a changed generated receiver. Upscaled dimensions were not new source detail. | Preserve artwork, hash and coverage, and validate receiver alignment. Record source, generation, crop or refine and delivery dimensions separately. REQ-010/012. |
| Clocks and ownership must compose once | Explicit delivery maps and padding checks caught wrong source frames. | Stale camera markers exposed an endcard early; shared camera actions were retimed twice; padding once included the following shot. | Separate story or native, solver, model and delivery time; traverse shared animation ownership and markers; decode actual padded media. REQ-008/010. |
| Recovery and review require actual output identity | A resumed generation reused completed states and refused a second driver. | Directory globbing reviewed inputs as outputs; a transferred symlink did not resolve remotely; a quiet or ambiguous job could not be assumed failed. | Durable input, graph, job and output hashes, exact output-node bindings and transfer verification precede criticism or retry. ## Hypotheses, not established runtime features

Source reviews proposed exact Blender vector and UV correspondence for temporal editors; sparse-anchor propagation through disocclusion; UV-baked or splat appearance reuse; learned stop-motion dynamics; general mouth and rig retargeting; live voice direction with reliable undo; and hosted motion-reference comparisons. Source inspection supported component possibilities but did not establish these end-to-end combinations.

Test one only when its claimed capability addresses a measured production failure. A fixed seed is not coherence; more anchors are not continuity; more solver links do not establish convergence; a still super-resolution panel does not establish temporal fidelity. Keep native rendering, dense image styling and guided video as empirical routes rather than assume one universal pipeline.

Judge improvement by accepted-shot yield, change-to-reviewed-result time, failures, manual interventions and full preparation, render, queue, retrieval and finishing cost. A short sampler time or a successful 30-second encode cannot substitute for a complete editable production that meets its brief.
