# Expectation workshop agenda

Use a workshop for shared or consequential business rules, not by default for
every small edit. Reuse existing acceptance and decision records.

1. Name the journey, authority and current domain vocabulary.
2. Have participants propose concrete success, boundary and failure examples.
3. Compare contradictions and exclusions before combining expectations.
4. Ask a participant other than the author to offer a falsifying input or effect.
5. Agree the evidence category, positive control, required independence and
   regression-critical scenarios.
6. Record resolved choices, authority already granted and remaining questions.
7. Assign actual owners and a bounded next step; do not claim logical role
   labels are executing workers.

Retain significant rejected alternatives and why they were rejected. If a
subjective judgement or device observation remains, identify who can supply it
and what an acceptable observation would contain.
