# Flow Nexus Platform

> ⚠️ **Not installed in this environment.** Requires Flow Nexus account.

## What it does

Manages Flow Nexus cloud platform resources:
- Account authentication and user management
- E2B sandbox creation and configuration
- App publishing to the Flow Nexus marketplace
- Credit system and billing management
- Coding challenge and leaderboard management

## To use this skill

Configure Flow Nexus credentials, then this skill becomes available.
